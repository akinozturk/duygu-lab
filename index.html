The Ozturk Research Group investigates the fundamental mechanisms of post-transcriptional regulation, focusing on how key protein complexes control mRNA translation, stability, and decay in development and disease.

Explore Our Work Recent Publications

Dr. Duygu Kuzuoglu Ozturk

Principal Investigator & Molecular Biologist

Dr. Ozturk leads the group with expertise in molecular and cell biology, focusing on the mechanisms that control mRNA fate and protein synthesis. Her work identifies crucial regulatory steps and protein complexes that, when dysregulated, contribute to diseases like cancer and neurological disorders.

Core Research Areas

Investigating regulatory networks governing mRNA expression and stability.

🧬

RNA Decay & CCR4-NOT

Mechanistically dissecting the CCR4-NOT complex, a key regulator of mRNA deadenylation. We study its structure, assembly, and role in miRNA-mediated target silencing.

🔬

Translational Control in Cancer

Investigating how oncogenes like N-myc hijack the translational machinery (e.g., eIF4F complex, mTOR) to promote cancer cell fitness and identifying inhibitors.

🩸

Molecular Disease Mechanisms

Exploring the role of metabolic enzymes (like PHGDH) and protein-protein interactions in disease etiology, including viral infections and tissue regeneration.
